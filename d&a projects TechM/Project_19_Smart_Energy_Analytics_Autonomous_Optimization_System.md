### **Project 19**  
**Project Title**: Smart Energy Analytics & Autonomous Optimization System  
**Project Description**:  
Built an advanced energy forecasting and anomaly detection platform with Snowflake, rich Looker BI monitoring, and Agentic AI for automated energy management recommendations.  

**Objective**:  
- Design robust IoT data pipelines with Airflow and Snowflake  
- Develop advanced time-series models and anomaly detection  
- Create real-time style monitoring dashboards in Looker BI  
- Deploy Agentic AI agents for optimization suggestions and alert handling  
- Implement forecasting and consumption simulation  
- Support sustainability reporting and carbon tracking  

**Dataset**:  
Individual Household Electric Power Consumption (UCI Machine Learning Repository) — one household's minute-level power consumption over ~4 years (2006–2010), with sub-metering by section of the house. Same dataset as Project 09; this version routes it through Snowflake and adds Looker BI monitoring dashboards plus an optimization agent. Free, no application required.  
Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/235/individual+household+electric+power+consumption (or mirrored at https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohamedjamyl/household-power-consumption-dataset)

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud & Data Warehouse**: GCP, Snowflake  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **BI & Visualization**: Looker BI  
- **Agentic AI**: Energy optimization agents  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Advanced Data Engineering, ML & Agentic AI project focusing on IoT and sustainability  
**Outcome**:  
Delivered an intelligent energy management platform with predictive, visual, and autonomous capabilities.
