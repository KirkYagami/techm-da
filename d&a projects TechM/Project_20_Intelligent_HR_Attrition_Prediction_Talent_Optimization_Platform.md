### **Project 20**  
**Project Title**: Intelligent HR Attrition Prediction & Talent Optimization Platform  
**Project Description**:  
Developed a comprehensive talent analytics solution using Snowflake, Looker BI, and Agentic AI to predict attrition and provide personalized retention and hiring recommendations.  

**Objective**:  
- Build secure HR data pipelines with Airflow feeding into Snowflake  
- Train advanced attrition and talent models using BigQuery ML  
- Design workforce intelligence dashboards in Looker BI  
- Implement Agentic AI agents for personalized retention strategies and talent acquisition support  
- Enable scenario planning and workforce forecasting  
- Maintain privacy and ethical AI standards  

**Dataset**:  
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance — 1,470 employee records with demographics, job role, satisfaction, income, and attrition label. Synthetic but realistic, created by IBM data scientists for training purposes. Same dataset as Project 10; this version routes it through Snowflake and adds Looker BI dashboards plus a retention-strategy agent. Free, no application required.  
Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud & Data Warehouse**: GCP, Snowflake  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **BI & Visualization**: Looker BI  
- **Agentic AI**: HR decision support agents  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Advanced Data Engineering, ML & Agentic AI project focusing on HR and people analytics  
**Outcome**:  
Created a modern talent intelligence platform that combines predictive analytics, enterprise BI, and AI-driven recommendations for strategic workforce management.
