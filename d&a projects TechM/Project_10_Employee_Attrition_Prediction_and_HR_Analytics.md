### **Project 10**  
**Project Title**: Employee Attrition Prediction & HR Analytics  
**Project Description**:  
Built a talent analytics pipeline to predict employee attrition risk and provide HR insights using automated data workflows and machine learning on GCP.  

**Objective**:  
- Implement Airflow-based HR data processing pipelines  
- Train attrition prediction models using BigQuery ML  
- Develop HR dashboards for workforce insights  
- Automate monthly prediction cycles  
- Support feature importance analysis  
- Ensure privacy-preserving design  

**Dataset**:  
IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance — 1,470 employee records with demographics, job role, satisfaction, income, and attrition label. Synthetic but realistic, created by IBM data scientists for training purposes. Free, no application required.  
Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/pavansubhasht/ibm-hr-analytics-attrition-dataset

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud Platform**: Google Cloud Platform (GCP), BigQuery  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **Visualization**: Looker Studio  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Intermediate Data Engineering & ML project focusing on HR analytics  
**Outcome**:  
Successfully created an employee attrition prediction system with actionable HR visualizations and insights.
