### **Project 15**  
**Project Title**: Intelligent Supply Chain Optimization Platform  
**Project Description**:  
Developed an advanced supply chain system featuring Snowflake as the analytics warehouse, sophisticated Looker BI visibility, and Agentic AI for automated decision-making in inventory and logistics.  

**Objective**:  
- Implement multi-system data integration pipelines with Airflow and Snowflake  
- Build optimization and forecasting models using BigQuery ML  
- Design executive and operational Looker BI dashboards  
- Deploy Agentic AI agents for automated reorder suggestions and risk mitigation  
- Enable end-to-end traceability and simulation capabilities  
- Support scenario planning and what-if analysis  

**Dataset**:  
Walmart Recruiting - Store Sales Forecasting — same weekly sales dataset as Projects 02, 05, and 12 (45 stores, holiday markdowns, fuel price, CPI, unemployment). As with Project 05, there's no single canonical public "supply chain inventory" dataset, so this project reuses Walmart's sales data as a proxy for demand and derives reorder/safety-stock logic from it. This version adds Snowflake, Looker BI, and a reorder-suggestion agent on top. Free, no application required.  
Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/aslanahmedov/walmart-sales-forecast

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud & Data Warehouse**: GCP, Snowflake  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **BI & Visualization**: Looker BI  
- **Agentic AI**: Supply chain decision agents  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Advanced Data Engineering, ML & Agentic AI project focusing on optimization and operations  
**Outcome**:  
Delivered a smart supply chain platform combining predictive analytics, enterprise BI, and autonomous AI capabilities.
