### **Project 17**  
**Project Title**: Advanced Patient Readmission Risk & Care Optimization System  
**Project Description**:  
Created an enhanced healthcare analytics platform using Snowflake, Looker BI, and Agentic AI to support clinical decisions with predictive risk scoring and personalized care recommendations.  

**Objective**:  
- Develop secure and compliant data pipelines with Airflow and Snowflake  
- Train sophisticated risk models using BigQuery ML  
- Build clinical and administrative Looker BI dashboards  
- Deploy Agentic AI agents for care plan generation and risk mitigation suggestions  
- Implement monitoring for model drift and data quality  
- Ensure privacy and regulatory compliance  

**Dataset**:  
Diabetes 130-US Hospitals for Years 1999-2008 (UCI Machine Learning Repository) — ~100,000 inpatient encounters with demographics, diagnoses, lab results, medications, and 30-day readmission label. Same dataset as Project 07; this version routes it through Snowflake and adds Looker BI dashboards plus a clinical decision-support agent. Free, no application required, fully de-identified.  
Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/296/diabetes+130-us+hospitals+for+years+1999-2008

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud & Data Warehouse**: GCP, Snowflake  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **BI & Visualization**: Looker BI  
- **Agentic AI**: Clinical decision support agents  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Advanced Data Engineering, ML & Agentic AI project focusing on healthcare analytics  
**Outcome**:  
Delivered a responsible and intelligent healthcare prediction platform with enterprise BI and AI assistance.
