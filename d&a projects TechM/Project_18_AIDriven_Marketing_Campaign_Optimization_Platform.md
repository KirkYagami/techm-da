### **Project 18**  
**Project Title**: AI-Driven Marketing Campaign Optimization Platform  
**Project Description**:  
Enhanced the marketing analytics system with Snowflake data unification, Looker BI executive dashboards, and Agentic AI for autonomous campaign optimization and budget allocation.  

**Objective**:  
- Orchestrate cross-channel data integration into Snowflake using Airflow  
- Build advanced attribution and predictive models  
- Create comprehensive Looker BI marketing intelligence dashboards  
- Implement Agentic AI agents for campaign strategy recommendations  
- Support automated performance optimization loops  
- Enable multi-touch attribution and incrementality analysis  

**Dataset**:  
Marketing Campaign Dataset (Jack Daoud / Maven Marketing) — 2,240 customers with channel response data (web, catalog, store purchases) and campaign acceptance across 5 historical campaigns. Same dataset as Project 08; this version unifies it in Snowflake and adds Looker BI dashboards plus a campaign-strategy agent. Free, no application required.  
Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rodsaldanha/arketing-campaign

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud & Data Warehouse**: GCP, Snowflake  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **BI & Visualization**: Looker BI  
- **Agentic AI**: Marketing optimization agents  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Advanced Data Engineering, ML & Agentic AI project focusing on marketing analytics  
**Outcome**:  
Implemented a modern marketing intelligence platform powered by data, BI, and intelligent agents.
