### **Project 09**  
**Project Title**: Energy Consumption Forecasting & Anomaly Detection  
**Project Description**:  
Created a smart energy analytics platform that forecasts consumption patterns and detects unusual usage through cloud-orchestrated pipelines and ML models.  

**Objective**:  
- Develop Airflow pipelines for smart meter and sensor data  
- Build time-series forecasting and anomaly detection models  
- Create real-time monitoring style dashboards (batch version)  
- Schedule daily forecast and alert generation  
- Implement data quality validation  
- Support reporting for energy optimization  

**Dataset**:  
Individual Household Electric Power Consumption (UCI Machine Learning Repository) — one household's minute-level power consumption over ~4 years (2006–2010), with sub-metering by section of the house. Free, no application required.  
Link: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/235/individual+household+electric+power+consumption (or mirrored at https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohamedjamyl/household-power-consumption-dataset)

**Tools Used**:  
- **Orchestration**: Apache Airflow  
- **Cloud Platform**: Google Cloud Platform (GCP), BigQuery  
- **ML Modeling**: BigQuery ML  
- **Visualization**: Looker Studio  
- **Languages & Utilities**: SQL, Python  

**Project Type**: Intermediate Data Engineering & ML project focusing on IoT and time-series analytics  
**Outcome**:  
Delivered an energy analytics solution capable of forecasting and identifying anomalies effectively.
